## Ideas for V1.1

*Worth considering once V1 has been used for a while.*

- Landscape is currently permitted on a layout designed portrait-first. Either
  commit to portrait-only or check the landscape layouts.
- Reopening a closed month, if closing one by accident turns out to hurt.

## Do Not Change Yet

*Deliberate V1 decisions. Do not revisit without real evidence from use.*

- **Manual entry.** The point of the app, not a missing integration.
- **No cloud sync.** Local-only is what makes the privacy claim true.
- **Plaintext backups.** Documented plainly; inventing cryptography would be
  worse than being honest.
- **Restore replaces rather than merges.** A merge has ambiguous cases that
  cannot be resolved without inventing rules about the user's own money.
- **Closed months stay closed.** The protection exists so a finished month
  cannot drift. Needs evidence of real pain before it changes.
- **Duplicate category names allowed.** Blocking them is a rule the user did
  not ask for.
