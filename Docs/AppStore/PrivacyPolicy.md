**Finance Notebook — Privacy Policy**

_Last updated: [DATE]_

Finance Notebook is a personal finance app that runs entirely on your iPhone.

**What we collect**

Nothing. Finance Notebook has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no
crash reporting. The developer receives no information about you or your use of
the app.

**Where your data is stored**

The information you enter — monthly plans, budgets, expenses, money added,
reviews and reflections — is stored on your device, in storage private to the
app. It is not uploaded to a server, and it is not synced to iCloud.

**No account**

Finance Notebook has no sign-in and no user account. No email address, name, or
identifier is collected or created.

**No bank connection**

Finance Notebook does not connect to any bank or financial institution. It never
asks for banking credentials, card numbers, or account numbers, and it cannot
import transactions. Everything in the app is entered by you.

**Network access**

The app makes no network requests of any kind.

**Files you export**

You can export a backup file or a PDF report. These files contain your financial
history and are **not encrypted**. When you export one, you choose where it goes
— Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, or another app — and once it leaves Finance
Notebook it is governed by wherever you sent it, not by this policy. Store these
files somewhere you trust.

**Deleting the app**

Because your data is stored only on your device, deleting Finance Notebook
deletes your notebook. Export a backup first if you want to keep it, or to move
to a new iPhone.

**Children**

Finance Notebook is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

**Changes**

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
