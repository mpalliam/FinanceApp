**Finance Notebook Support**

**How do I start a month?**
Open the app and tap *Start a Month*. Enter the money you have available and the
amount you want to protect — money you don't intend to spend. You can add
category budgets afterwards from the Plan tab.

**What is protected money?**
An amount you set aside from what's spendable. It is subtracted from your
starting money when working out what's safe to spend, so it stays out of the
way rather than being budgeted.

**How do I record spending?**
Expenses are entered by hand, from the Transactions tab. Finance Notebook does
not connect to a bank, so nothing appears on its own.

**What if I forget to categorise something?**
The Plan tab shows an Uncategorized section when spending has no category. It
lets you select several expenses and assign them all at once.

**How do backups work?**
Settings → Export Backup writes a `.financebackup` file containing your whole
notebook, and lets you save or send it wherever you like. It is a plain,
unencrypted file, so keep it somewhere you trust.

**How do I restore?**
Settings → Restore Backup, then pick the file. The backup is checked before
anything happens, and you are shown what it contains before you confirm.
Restoring **replaces** your current notebook rather than merging into it.

**How do I move to a new iPhone?**
Export a backup on the old phone, get the file to the new one — AirDrop, Files
or iCloud Drive — then install Finance Notebook and restore it.

**Can I reopen a closed month?**
Not in this version. Closing a month is how a finished month is protected from
drifting after the fact.

**Does Finance Notebook connect to my bank?**
No, and it cannot. There is no bank integration and the app makes no network
requests. It never asks for banking credentials.

**Where is my data stored?**
On your iPhone, in storage private to the app. It isn't uploaded anywhere and
isn't synced to iCloud. Deleting the app deletes that data, so export a backup
first.

**What happens to a PDF report?**
It's a report, not a backup — it can't be read back into the app. Use Export
Backup for anything you want to restore later.
